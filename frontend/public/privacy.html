FRAGGED Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated · 10 June 2026

FRAGGED ("we", "the site") operates csstat.com, a free Counter-Strike 2 stats viewer. You paste a Steam ID or profile URL and we show public stats pulled live from Steam, Leetify, and Faceit. This page explains what data is involved and how advertising works.

What we collect

FRAGGED has no accounts, no login, and no database. We do not ask for or store your name, email, or password, and we do not build a profile of you.

  • The Steam ID you search. When you look up a player, the ID or vanity name you enter is sent to Steam, Leetify, and Faceit so we can fetch that player's public stats. We do not retain it after the request.
  • Standard server logs. Like most websites, our hosting (Cloudflare) processes basic technical request data such as IP address and browser type to deliver the site and protect against abuse.

Advertising & cookies

FRAGGED uses Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to FRAGGED and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized ads at aboutads.info/choices.
  • You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Third-party services

The stats shown on FRAGGED come from public APIs. Your search is shared with these providers, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Steam (Valve)
  • Leetify
  • Faceit
  • Google (AdSense)
  • Cloudflare (hosting)

Data retention

We do not store player stats or search history on our own servers — every lookup is fetched live and discarded after it is returned to your browser.

Children

FRAGGED is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
